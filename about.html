The short version

  1. I build software for a living, mostly on my own.
  2. About twenty apps and websites in roughly two years.
  3. Some are courier platforms, some are messengers, and all of them shipped.
  4. I like the unglamorous parts: signaling, sync and offline behavior, the details that decide whether an app feels solid.
  5. Then I like making the front of it feel alive.
  6. I'm in Dhaka, and I have room for another project.

What I do

Whole products.

Backend, web and Android from one person, so nothing gets lost in a handoff.

Real-time and AI features.

Chat and calling over WebRTC, with the signaling, push notifications and local storage around them, plus Gemini-powered smart replies and summaries.

Interactive front ends.

WebGL, shaders and motion where they help, like the WebGL hero on this page.

behind the work
