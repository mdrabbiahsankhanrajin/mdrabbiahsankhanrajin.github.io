Lumina

A production Android chat and calling app, built natively — Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Firebase, and WebRTC, no cross-platform compromise.

The problem

Cross-platform frameworks make it easy to skip learning what a target platform actually offers. Lumina exists to prove native-platform range beyond JavaScript: a real, production Android chat and calling app built directly against the platform, not a wrapper around it.

My part

Solo founder & developer — architecture, UI in Kotlin/Compose, and the WebRTC calling integration.

Stack

  • Kotlin
  • Jetpack Compose
  • Firebase
  • WebRTC

Outcome

A production Android chat and calling app shipped end to end in Kotlin/Compose with Firebase and WebRTC — evidence of native-platform depth alongside the JS/TS work the rest of the portfolio leans on.
