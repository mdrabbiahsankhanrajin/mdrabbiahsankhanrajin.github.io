Cloud POS platform

SmartPOS BD

A cloud-based point-of-sale, inventory and business-management platform for small and medium businesses in Bangladesh.

Status
Cloud POS platform
Focus
Point of sale, Inventory, Multi-branch

What it is

SmartPOS BD brings checkout, stock, purchasing, customers, expenses and reporting into one secure system for retail stores, pharmacies, restaurants, fashion stores and wholesalers.

What is in it

  • Point of sale with barcode and instant product search, discounts, tax, multiple payment methods and receipt printing
  • Inventory with live stock, low-stock alerts, adjustments, product variants and branch-level stock, plus purchasing and supplier management
  • Sales history, controlled refund approval with stock restoration, customer profiles with outstanding balances, and expense tracking
  • Profit-and-loss, cash-flow, inventory and sales reports with exports; owner, manager and cashier roles; multi-branch operations; subscription plans and an audit trail
