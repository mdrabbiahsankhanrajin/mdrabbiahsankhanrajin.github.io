Parcel X

A mobile-first courier delivery platform for Bangladesh that brings customers, riders and administrators into one system.

Status
Courier platform for Bangladesh
Focus
Mobile-first, Role-based access, Real-time

What it is

Parcel X covers booking parcels, tracking deliveries, managing rider jobs and monitoring operations. Customers, riders and administrators each get their own protected area.

What is in it

  • Customers create delivery requests, choose package types, see price estimates and follow each parcel from pending to delivered or cancelled
  • Riders see available jobs, accept or reject them, update parcel status and track completed work and earnings
  • Administrators review orders, revenue and riders, manage users and rider access, assign parcels and manage pricing
  • Secure sign-in with role-based access, real-time parcel updates, and an offline notice with retry-friendly behaviour

Status

Screens shown: sign-in, sign-up, delivery pricing for Dhaka, nearby areas and nationwide, public parcel tracking, and password recovery.
