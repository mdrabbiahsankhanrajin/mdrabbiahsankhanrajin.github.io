Connect

A native Android messenger with encrypted chat, WebRTC audio and video calls signalled through Firestore, and Gemini-powered smart replies and summaries — roughly 20,000 lines of Kotlin, built solo.

Year
2026
Role
Solo founder & developer
Built with
Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Firebase, WebRTC, Gemini

The problem

Chat, calling and AI assistance usually arrive as separate products bolted together. Connect was built to put all three in one native Android app that a single developer owns end to end: direct and group messaging, audio and video calls, and AI helpers for replying, summarising and polishing messages — with message text encrypted on the device before it reaches the backend.

My part

Solo founder & developer — designed and built the app end to end: the Jetpack Compose UI across nine screens, the Firestore data layer with a Room cache, the WebRTC calling stack with Firestore signaling, the message-encryption layer, and the Gemini-powered AI features.

Stack

  • Kotlin
  • Jetpack Compose
  • Material 3
  • Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Storage, FCM)
  • Room
  • WebRTC
  • Firebase AI Logic (Gemini)
  • AES-256-GCM

Outcome

A working 1.0 Android app of roughly 20,000 lines of Kotlin: direct and group chat with replies, reactions, edit and delete, pinned and starred messages, in-chat search and voice notes; audio and video calls over WebRTC with Firestore signaling and call history; presence and typing indicators; Google and email sign-in; and Gemini-powered smart replies, conversation summaries and message polish. Message text is AES-256-GCM encrypted client-side, and that layer has its own unit tests.
