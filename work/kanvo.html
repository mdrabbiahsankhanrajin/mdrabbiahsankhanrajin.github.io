Kanvo

A proposed all-in-one content workspace for businesses, creators and marketing teams: write, design, plan, publish and measure in one app.

Status
Prototype
Focus
Product concept, Dark editorial UI, Prototype

What it is

Kanvo's idea is to replace the juggling of separate writing, design, scheduling and analytics tools with one workspace. The visual prototype uses a dark editorial look with coral accents, serif headings and a persistent sidebar.

What is in it

  • Home page introducing the product with a workspace preview
  • Dashboard, AI Studio layout with Post, Image and Video modes, monthly content calendar and library
  • Brand kit with colours, typography, voice and logo examples, plus analytics, team and settings screens
  • Roadmap: AI post, image and video generation, a content calendar and one-click publishing first, then analytics, brand kit, collaboration and an AI marketing assistant

Status

Status: a visual prototype, not a finished product. Navigation works and you can pick a content type and enter a brief, but live AI generation is not connected. Metrics, calendar entries, team members and the library tiles are demo content; sign-in, saved data, publishing and billing are not implemented.

Focus
