Storefront prototype

Rannaghor Smart

A mobile-first online store for practical kitchen tools, designed for Bangladeshi households.

Status
Storefront prototype
Focus
E-commerce, Cash on Delivery, bKash

What it is

The promise is cook smarter, not harder. The store is built around a conversion-focused homepage, a flagship product page, local payment choices and clear trust messaging, in a warm terracotta and cream identity.

What is in it

  • Homepage with a bestseller offer, trust signals and a featured flagship product
  • Product page with an image gallery, benefit-led copy, quantity selector and live order total
  • Bangladesh district selection with delivery pricing, and Cash on Delivery or bKash advance
  • Ratings, returns, warranty and delivery messaging, plus reviews, FAQs, bundles and repeat-order prompts

Status

Status: an interactive prototype. Order confirmation is demonstrated in the interface and is not connected to order storage or live payments, and the contact details are placeholders.

Focus

  • E-commerce
  • Mobile-first
  • Cash on Delivery
  • bKash
