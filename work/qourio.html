Qourio

B2B courier & delivery merchant SaaS platform for Bangladesh — a full production system, not a demo.

Year
2025
Role
Solo founder & developer
Built with
React, Vite, TypeScript, Supabase, Firebase, Capacitor

The problem

Bangladesh's courier and delivery merchants were running logistics through spreadsheets and phone calls — no unified platform to manage shipments, track deliveries, or reconcile cash-on-delivery payments in one place.

My part

Solo founder & developer — product, engineering, and the Play Store submission end to end.

Stack

  • React
  • Vite
  • TypeScript
  • Supabase
  • Firebase
  • Capacitor (Android)

Outcome

A production B2B merchant platform shipped end to end: a full bilingual English/Bengali i18n system spanning 29 translation namespaces, and a Capacitor-wrapped Android app with a completed Google Play Store submission pipeline. A smart-paste assistant turns a customer's pasted order message into a draft the merchant reviews before anything is created.
